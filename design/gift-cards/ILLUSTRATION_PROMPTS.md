# Pina Market illustration prompts

Original graphic direction: matte paper, calm forest green and cream, soft studio light. Transparent backgrounds. Add all UI text and amounts as editable text in Figma. No logos, card chips, real QR codes, crypto or bank transfer routes. These prompts describe decorative illustrations, not working payment instruments.

## HERO

HERO — главная, 1600×1200, соотношение 4:3, прозрачный фон. Prompt: “Create an editorial product illustration for Pina Market, a marketplace for merchant-specific gift cards used to prepay goods and services abroad. Two large matte paper gift cards standing on a low cream pedestal, a small abstract storefront shape behind them and one restrained curved green ribbon connecting the composition. Cards have only abstract geometric marks, no chip and no readable content. Forest green #1E7A46, deep green #163B2A, pale lime #DCEEBA, warm white. Soft daylight, subtle contact shadows, tactile paper, premium but approachable, clean silhouette, centered composition, generous transparent margins. This is a prepaid merchant gift card, not a bank card.” На десктопе иллюстрация справа, текст слева; на телефоне под текстом. Само изображение не содержит текстовую половину экрана.

## BUYER

BUYER — преимущества, 1200×900, 4:3, прозрачный фон. Prompt: “A small matte paper card emerging from a clean abstract order folder, with a simple open doorway in the background suggesting access to a chosen merchant. Calm editorial still life, no people, no currency, no bank transfer arrows. Same forest green, pale lime and warm cream palette, soft daylight, rounded geometry, tactile paper, restrained detail, centered isolated objects with wide margins.”

## MERCHANT

MERCHANT — страница поставщиков, 1600×1200, 4:3, прозрачный фон. Prompt: “An approachable modern merchant workspace in a minimal paper sculpture style: a small storefront facade, neatly arranged order folders and three distinct merchant gift cards in a tray. Communicate a practical new sales channel and organized orders, without charts or money piles. Forest green and cream with pale lime accents, soft daylight, subtle shadows, clean centered composition, no lettering or brands.”

## STEPS

STEPS — три шага, 1800×600, 3:1, прозрачный фон. Prompt: “Create one horizontal triptych of three equally spaced small editorial paper sculptures: left, a gift card selected from three cards; center, a folded invoice document beside a generic bank counter symbol; right, a gift card beside a simple storefront with a small confirmation seal. Maintain identical scale, baseline, lighting and green cream palette across the three groups. Large empty gaps between groups so each group can be cropped separately. No text, no step numbers, no arrows crossing groups, no secret code content.” Вёрстка показывает отдельные сцены и реальные подписи. Порядок на телефоне вертикальный.

## ACCESS

ACCESS — вход и защищённый просмотр, 1000×800, 5:4, прозрачный фон. Prompt: “A simple closed card sleeve with a small geometric lock and an envelope, matte paper sculptural editorial style, calm forest green and warm cream, soft daylight and generous negative space. Communicate private account access, not insurance, government certification or guaranteed merchant performance. No shield badges with text.”

## EMPTY

EMPTY — пустой кабинет, 800×600, 4:3, прозрачный фон. Prompt: “A neatly open empty paper tray with one small blank card outline resting beside it, friendly minimal editorial illustration, muted forest green and cream, very low visual complexity, soft contact shadow, isolated transparent background, no sadness, no people, no lettering.” Использовать вместе с текстом «Здесь появятся ваши карты» и кнопкой «Выбрать карту».

## SUPPORT

SUPPORT — поддержка, 1000×800, 5:4, прозрачный фон. Prompt: “Two rounded matte paper conversation bubbles beside a small order folder, warm calm editorial still life, forest green and pale lime on cream, soft studio daylight, simple silhouettes, isolated transparent background. Communicate help with an order, no headset person, no phone number, no lettering, no promises of round-the-clock service.”
