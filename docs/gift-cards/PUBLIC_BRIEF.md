# Pina Market website design concept

Figma: https://www.figma.com/design/rIyrCBWcvMRjqTlE4RhHtA
Branch: codex/gift-cards-redesign

## Public positioning

Buyer headline: «Покупайте у зарубежных партнёров с оплатой в рублях».

Buyer description: «Подарочные карты компаний для оплаты товаров и услуг за рубежом. Выберите партнёра и номинал, оплатите заказ и получите код в личном кабинете, когда он понадобится».

Buyer benefits: оплата в рублях; код в нужный момент после подтверждения оплаты; карты, статусы и документы в кабинете.

Merchant headline: «Продавайте клиентам из России через карты вашей компании».

Merchant description: «Pina Market помогает покупателям приобрести ваши карты в рублях, а вам — принимать их в счёт своих товаров и услуг».

Main actions: «Выбрать карту», «Подключить компанию».

## Page composition

Home: headline and card illustration; benefits; choose/pay/use steps; actual partner programmes or an honest empty catalogue; merchant invitation; FAQ; company and legal links.

Merchant page: clear value statement, practical benefits and application form. Order: selected company and rules, denomination, total, email, available payment method and informed acceptance of terms. Account: orders and cards with clear statuses and support access.

The current Figma file includes these four pages in desktop and mobile layouts, additional concept states, a sheet of24 original editable icons and seven illustration prompts. Mock orders are labelled demonstration data. Images do not represent bank cards or guaranteed merchant performance. No invented partner logos, reviews or turnover statistics.

## Graphic files

`design/gift-cards/icons/` contains24 SVGs. `icons-sprite.svg` exposes symbols such as `pina-gift-card`; `icons-sheet.svg` is a complete6×4 transparent sheet; `manifest.json` lists stable names and positions. `ILLUSTRATION_PROMPTS.md` contains reusable generation prompts. Raster illustrations have not been generated.

This commit delivers design materials. It does not change the production PHP application or enable live gift-card payments. The complete operational specification is maintained separately and is not included in this public design package.
